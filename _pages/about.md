I am a Ph.D. student in Economics at the University of California, Riverside, specializing in advanced econometrics. I expect to complete my Ph.D. in June 2027. I also hold an M.S. in Computer Science from UC Riverside, awarded in June 2026.

My research interests lie at the intersection of **econometrics, machine learning, and financial economics**. My current work studies tail-risk pricing using structured mixture density networks, comparable-firm selection with language models, and deep learning for financial forecasting.

Before my doctoral studies, I worked in investment banking at Guosen Securities and Dongxing Securities, and as a Senior Associate in audit at PwC. I am also a CFA® charterholder.

I am seeking full-time opportunities in data science and related research roles.
